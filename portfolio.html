PIXEL ARTWORKS

BRYAN JOSEPH DE GUZMAN

BSCS - Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

I possess a profound dedication to the craft of pixel art, utilizing it as a medium for both technical expression and creative world-building.

My primary focus lies in the meticulous design of distinct characters, ensuring that every sprite extends beyond mere visual representation. I am passionate about constructing comprehensive lore, deep context, and engaging narratives for each creation.

I firmly believe that every pixel placed serves a fundamental purpose in telling a larger, interconnected story.
