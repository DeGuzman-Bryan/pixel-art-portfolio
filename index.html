DE GUZMAN'S ARTWORKS

BRYAN JOSEPH DE GUZMAN

BSCS - Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Welcome to my official digital portfolio. This web application serves as an interactive platform designed to demonstrate my technical capabilities, creative methodology, and multidisciplinary projects across software engineering and digital arts.

As a Computer Science scholar, my academic foundation focuses on software development, algorithmic design, and interactive media architecture. I specialize in integrating computer science principles with digital creativity—encompassing pixel art asset generation, 2D sprite animation, audio composition, and visual photography.

My objective is to bridge the gap between technical rigor and artistic expression. Every digital asset, frame of animation, soundwave, and captured photographic composition within this portfolio is systematically crafted to contribute to cohesive world-building, functional user experience, and immersive digital storytelling.

--- CHARACTERS & HUMANS ---

Char: Old Resident

Class: Town Elder / Civilian

Context: A peaceful town resident recognized by his classic yellow shirt, brown trousers, and white mustache.

Lore: Having spent decades witnessing the village grow, he offers wise advice and old folklore to traveling adventurers passing through.

Char: Khrixia

Class: Novice Spellweaver / Adventurer

Context: A spirited young mage who travels across realms to study ancient elemental relics and magical arts.

Lore: Recognized by her crimson hair ribbon and energetic demeanor, Khrixia possesses a natural affinity for magic and an unyielding curiosity for unexplored dungeons.

Char: Northern Barbarian

Class: Berserker / Tribal Warrior

Context: A fearless warrior hailing from the frozen northern peaks, equipped with heavy armor plates and a battle-worn greataxe.

Lore: Raised in unforgiving wilderness conditions, this hardened fighter channels primal fury in combat to shatter frontline defenses.

Char: Shadow Shinobi

Class: Assassin / Master Ninja

Context: An elusive warrior clad in dark garb and a crimson headband, carrying a lethal back-slung katana.

Lore: Trained in mist-shrouded mountain monasteries, this silent operative strikes from the shadows before adversaries even notice their presence.

Char: Arcane Scientist

Class: Alchemist / Researcher

Context: An eccentric scholar clad in a pristine white lab coat and protective goggles, holding a flask of volatile green liquid.

Lore: Dedicated to unlocking the secrets of elemental alchemy, he spends long nights concocting experimental potions to aid heroes on their journeys.

Char: Jerick & Wyngard

Class: Vanguard Duo (Paladin & Ranger)

Context: An armored warrior in blue plate steel (Jerick) and a nimble forest scout clad in a green cloak (Wyngard) bound by a sworn brotherhood.

Lore: Standing shoulder-to-shoulder through endless perilous ventures, Jerick provides an impenetrable frontline shield while Wyngard delivers swift, precision marksmanship from the flanks.

Type: Mythical Beast

Context: Rises from ancient burial grounds and ruined crypts, wandering aimlessly in search of living prey.

Context: Soars through cloudlit mountain peaks and enchanted sky sanctuaries, far above the reach of terrestrial predators.

Type: Legendary Beast

Context: Inhabits enchanted starlight glades and sacred ancient forests, guarded by arcane barriers.
